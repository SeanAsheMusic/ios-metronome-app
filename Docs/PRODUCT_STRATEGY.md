# Product Strategy

## Positioning

The app should behave like a hardware metronome on first launch and grow into a rhythm workstation without app-switching. The main competitive wedge is trust: no ads, no account, no subscription-gated core features, and no copied audio assets.

## Differentiation

- Stage-readable default surface.
- Musically correct separation of tempo, meter, grouping, subdivision, and accents.
- Local-first library with export/import before optional sync.
- Rights-clean synthesized click library.
- Accessibility treated as core quality.

## Contradictions Resolved

The research describes a broad ideal product including tuner, practice suite, sync, Link, Watch, and analytics. V1 intentionally narrows scope to protect timing quality and data trust. Advanced practice and connectivity features are deferred until the core click, model, persistence, and accessibility foundation is strong.
